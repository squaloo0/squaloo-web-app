The vision to scale

Ask scales to task. Edge scales to cloud.

The layer that makes an answer verifiable is the layer that will let an agent act on the floor. Make a small model behave offline, on hardware a plant owns, and the same discipline carries up to large models in the cloud. Compute, models and hardware are someone else’s race; the data from AI deployed where it matters, in its owner’s custody, is nobody’s yet. Solomon is the middleware that makes it collectable. Squaloo orchestrates the measurement across sites, models and regulators, with every customer’s content stripped.

How we build

A few exact rules, applied without exception.

The geometers who built the great patterns worked with a compass, a straightedge and a handful of rules, and the culture they belonged to gave the world the word algorithm. What we take from them is the method, not the ornament. Keep the rules few, keep them exact, apply them without exception, and the result is correct; correct things tend to be beautiful, and beautiful things tend to feel safe, because they are. Solomon is built the same way: a short list of rules the engine never breaks, measured every night, published with the misses.

The receipt

Every answer carries its sources. The engine attaches them; the model cannot author them.

The ruler

A published contract a model must satisfy, measured nightly on hardware we own, with the method to recompute every figure.

The record

Results published unedited, misses included. Where we were wrong, the correction is a dated row.

Measured

Don’t take our word for it.

The ruler runs every night and the numbers land here, dated and scoped. Anyone can re-run them.

Where we came from

An inside-out view, withered technology, and a theory about dumb models.

THE SPIRAL · INSIDE OUT
Our ethos starts on the inside and works outward: the line a hand draws while it is thinking. Squaloo means “do you.” The method came before the product.
WITHERED TECHNOLOGY
Mature, cheap, well-understood parts, used laterally: a $100 computer, a small open model, the export the controller already makes. Nothing exotic has to be true for Solomon to work.
DUMB MODEL THEORY
Low-compute models doing jobs for bigger models, the way algebra builds the algorithm from simple parts. Small, exact, composable, and easy to put at the edge. That idea became Solomon.
NOW · SOLOMON
The floor, where owning it and verifying it are already the rule. Any surface, any machine, and a ruler that travels with the model.
