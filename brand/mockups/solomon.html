HOW IT WORKS

One engine,three custody zones.

The engine runs on a body at the site edge, offline, on hardware the customer owns: it captures what the veteran says, reads the export the controller already produces, and answers with the receipts attached. The customer’s tenant holds the Brain, where every word and every reading lives under the customer’s keys. Squaloo keeps only the measurement, content stripped. Any model that passes the ruler on the body, unmodified, is admissible; two already have.

THE VISION FOR THE PRODUCT

Ask scales totask.Edge scales to cloud.

A verified answer today. A verified action next, with the same receipts. The layer that makes an answer verifiable is the layer that lets an agent act on the floor, and nothing about that layer depends on which model is behind it. Make a small model behave offline on a $100 body and the discipline carries up to large models in the cloud.
