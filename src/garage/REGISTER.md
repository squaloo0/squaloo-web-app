| At this site's traffic, a statistically significant change in a metric is presumed to overstate its size, and to have a real chance of the wrong sign, until a second window repeats it. | A banked prediction's stopping rule depends only on data already seen, and the evidence is aggregated over all data to the stop; the result is read as a posterior with a prior stated in the row.
